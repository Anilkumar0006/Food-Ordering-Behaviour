A Structured Analysis of Choices and Habits

This project presents an interactive analysis of customer food ordering behaviour using Tableau dashboards and storytelling techniques. The analysis identifies customer preferences, ordering patterns, popular cuisines, payment trends and consumer habits using real-world food ordering data.

Project Information

This website showcases an end-to-end analysis of food ordering behaviour using interactive Tableau visualizations. The project focuses on identifying customer ordering habits, preferred cuisines, age-wise behaviour, spending trends, payment methods and consumer insights.

About the Project

Understanding food ordering behaviour has become increasingly important for restaurants, food delivery platforms, and businesses. This project analyzes customer ordering patterns, food preferences, spending habits, and consumer trends through interactive Tableau dashboards and data storytelling.

Food Ordering Behaviour and Consumer Trends: A Structured Analysis of Choices and Habits

The project transforms raw food ordering data into meaningful business insights using Tableau dashboards. It helps identify customer preferences, purchasing behaviour, popular cuisines, ordering frequency, payment methods, demographic patterns, and consumer spending habits.

Key Insights

The analysis highlights significant customer ordering trends, preferences and spending behaviour.

Customer Preferences

Customers tend to order their favourite cuisines repeatedly, indicating strong food preferences.

Spending Behaviour

Consumer spending patterns vary significantly across different age groups and ordering frequencies.

Digital Payments

Digital payment methods dominate customer transactions, reflecting the increasing adoption of online payments.

Ordering Frequency

Frequent customers contribute significantly to overall food ordering activities.

Consumer Trends
